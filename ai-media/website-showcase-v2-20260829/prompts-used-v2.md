# Prompts Used — Creative Website Showcase v2 (product 211, 2026-08-29)

One native-generation call per style. Every prompt shared the same skeleton:

1. **Frame** — "A frame from a luxury cinema commercial", square 1:1, mood line per style.
2. **Product paragraph (identical in all six)** — men's 925 sterling silver ring, large
   oval purple amethyst cabochon, crown of pointed silver prongs, beaded bezel, ornate
   floral hand-engraved tapering band; preserve exact silhouette, proportions, amethyst
   shape/color/internal character, engravings; reference camera angle (slightly above,
   front three-quarter); "same product, never a redesign"; hero in razor focus.
3. **Scene paragraph (per style)** —
   - 01: black void, silver smoke veils behind/around (never over the stone), single
     spotlight on the gemstone, cold rim light, smoke-softened reflection + contact shadow.
   - 02: charcoal scene, out-of-focus gold dust and ember bokeh, cracked dark slab,
     amber key left / shadow right, no fire, no hue shift on the stone.
   - 03: wet black mirror with micro raindrops around (never on the stone), blue-graphite
     gradient, far city-light bokeh, crisp fading reflection, one warm accent glint.
   - 04: weathered dark stone with abstract carved Persian GEOMETRIC relief (no script,
     no symbols), warm god-ray through a high window, floating dust motes, deep shadows.
   - 05: levitation a few centimeters above a dark reflective pedestal, soft halo
     backlight, out-of-focus silver-grey rock fragments never overlapping the ring,
     precise shadow proving the hover; product itself photorealistic and unchanged.
   - 06: deep emerald crushed velvet with theatrical folds, ring cradled in a fold,
     theatrical spotlight upper-left, folds frame without hiding, no green cast on stone.
4. **Composition** — ring ~55% of frame, fully visible, cinematic negative space,
   plausible shadows/reflections, accurate metal rendering.
5. **Global negatives (from the brief)** — different/redesigned ring, altered gemstone,
   changed color/pattern, invented engraving, extra/missing stones, plastic metal,
   floating/broken/cropped ring, rough cutout, pasted look, halo artifacts, impossible
   reflection, human, hand, fingers, mannequin, text, price, watermark, logo, pink/
   purple background, hearts, roses, glitter, kitsch, cartoon, CGI look, oversaturated.

No text overlays, no logos, no post-generation compositing. Native 1024×1024 →
Lanczos 1600×1600, WebP q93 + PNG, sRGB.
