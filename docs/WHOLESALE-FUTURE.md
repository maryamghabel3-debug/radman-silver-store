# چشم‌انداز و زیرساخت فروش عمده B2B (`WHOLESALE-FUTURE.md`)

This document outlines the future B2B wholesale expansion architecture for partner jewelry shops and galleries across Iran.

---

## 1. Wholesale Tiered Pricing Model (`مدل قیمت‌گذاری عمده‌فروشی`)

- **Tier 1 — Gallery Partner (`5 to 19 Pieces`):** 15% wholesale discount off retail calculated price.
- **Tier 2 — Regional Distributor (`20+ Pieces`):** Wholesale base silver gram rate + flat silversmithing craftsmanship fee (no retail brand margin applied).

---

## 2. B2B Wholesale Portal Architecture (`پورتال همکاران تجاری`)

- **Custom User Role:** `Wholesale_Partner` role in WooCommerce.
- **Verification Workflow:** Jewelry shop owners upload their valid Iranian commercial business license (`جواز کسب طلا و جواهر`) via a dedicated B2B registration form.
- **Wholesale Display:** Authenticated wholesale partners see wholesale gram rates and bulk order quantities directly on product detail pages.
